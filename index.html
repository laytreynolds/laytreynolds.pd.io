Frequently Asked Questions

What networks does Purple Direct use?
Purple Direct supports all major UK networks including EE, Vodafone, and O2. You'll get the same great coverage at a fraction of the cost.
How quickly will I receive my SIM card?
Most orders are delivered within 1-2 business days. Many customers receive their SIM card the very next day after ordering.
Can I keep my existing phone number?
Yes! You can easily transfer your existing number to Purple Direct. We'll guide you through the simple PAC code process.
Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime?
All our plans are rolling monthly — no long-term contracts. You can cancel or change your plan at any time with no penalties.
Do your plans include 5G?
Yes, all of our plans include 5G access at no extra cost, wherever 5G coverage is available on your chosen network.
